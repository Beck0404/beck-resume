執照獸醫，走出診間投入伴侶動物產業。做過經銷商的顧問獸醫、動物醫院通路的技術行銷、品牌商的產品經理，到製造商的行銷經理與產品開發經理。職涯三個篇章，一個邏輯：把專業轉化成獸醫師與飼主都聽得懂的價值。A licensed veterinarian who left the clinic for the companion-animal industry — consultant vet at a distributor, technical marketing in the veterinary-hospital channel, product manager at a brand, then marketing manager and product development manager at a manufacturer. Three chapters, one logic: turn expertise into value that vets and pet owners understand.

01

臨床訓練Clinical Training

台大五年臨床養成：從症狀、檢驗與飼主描述建立真相。這套診斷思維，後來成了跟獸醫師溝通產品的共同語言。Five years of clinical training at NTU — building truth from symptoms, tests and what families describe. That diagnostic mindset became the shared language for talking products with veterinarians.

02

通路、教育與技術行銷Channel, Education & Technical Marketing

Farmina、Dr. Clauder's 顧問獸醫，為動物醫院與寵物店做產品教育；再到動物醫院通路做再生醫學的技術行銷。學會把實驗數據變成診間與貨架都能用的故事。Consultant vet for Farmina and Dr. Clauder's, training veterinary hospitals and retailers; then technical marketing of regenerative medicine in the vet-hospital channel. Learned to turn lab data into stories that work in the clinic and on the shelf.

03

品牌行銷到產品開發Brand Marketing to Product Development
